Notes

The winner is not a strict Condorcet winner. The Order of Time wins the Borda count outright, and beats seven of its eight rivals head-to-head — but loses to The Black Count 5–6. Borda is the club's stated method and it produced a clean winner with a four-point margin, so this is a curiosity rather than a dispute. It is recorded here because the pairwise table is part of the round.

Consensus over passion. The Order of Time took just one first-place vote and no last-place finishes; it won on the strength of eleven middling-to-high placements. Say Nothing led the field on first-place votes with three and still finished sixth. The Order of Time placed seventh in the September poll on 38 points.

Mean rank cannot break a Borda tie. The three tied books show identical mean ranks of 4.64 — necessarily, since Borda points are a linear function of rank (points = 10 − rank, so equal totals force equal means). Mean rank is a restatement of the Borda score, not an independent breaker, and it will tie every time the score does.

Methodology

Borda count with 9 candidates: 1st place = 9 points, last place = 1 point. Eleven forced-rank ballots collected via survey, 12–16 July 2026. Ties resolved per the club's Polling Methodology memo (formalized after the February 2026 tie): Condorcet head-to-head first, then first-place votes if pairwise is tied. No tie occurred at the top of this round, so no tiebreaker was applied to the selection.
