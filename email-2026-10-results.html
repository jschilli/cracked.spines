§ About that three-way tie
Easy Money, The Laws of Thought and The Black Count all finished on 59 points. The methodology calls for a Condorcet head-to-head — and this time it has nothing to offer, because the three of them form a perfect loop. Each beats one and loses to the other, every margin 6–5:
Head-to-head
Easy Money 6 · Laws of Thought 5
Head-to-head
Laws of Thought 6 · Black Count 5
Head-to-head
Black Count 6 · Easy Money 5
Rock, paper, scissors. Happily nothing hinged on it — the tie is for third, all three stay on the candidate list, and the field of nine means no relegations. So we are recording a three-way third and moving on. Worth one more note in the interest of full disclosure: The Order of Time won the Borda count outright but is not a strict Condorcet winner — it beats seven of its eight rivals head-to-head and loses to The Black Count 5–6. Borda is our stated method and it named a winner cleanly, so the pairwise quirk is a curiosity, not a dispute.
